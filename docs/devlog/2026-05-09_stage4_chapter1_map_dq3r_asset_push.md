# Stage 4 Chapter 1 Map DQ3R Asset Push

Date: 2026-05-09
Branch: `codex/stage4-graphics-foundation-20260508`

## Scope

This pass pushes the Chapter 1 Antela review assets beyond the 19-FBX map batch by adding camera-framed close-density modules, a reusable modular detail kit, an authored-look surface atlas pack, and additional Blender review captures. The initial generation pass did not launch Unity; a graphics-foundation follow-up imported the expanded set into review prefabs without opening or saving a production scene.

The goal is not to copy a specific commercial title. The target is a stricter HD-2D read: clear foreground / midground / background depth, denser roof and wall construction, ground contact information, walkable-band readability, Current/Past material-state contrast, and Time Window floor adjacency that remains a thin visual trace instead of a gate or physical opening.

## HD-2D Read

S3 Current now has additional roof edges, eave undersides, plaster cracks, wall patches, curb stones, drain grooves, dust breakup, Time Window ground traces, a 1.5m character anchor, and a review-only walkable band.

S3 Past adds market rope lines, hanging folds, baskets, denser spice jars, Dario / Kairo / Luna character anchors, and a same-coordinate walk band so Current/Past differences read as lived-in activity rather than palette swap.

S4 Current/Past add tree-base roots, field grounding shadows, seed sacks, tool silhouettes, fallen nuts, leaf scatter, Dario spice-sightline traces, and Kaia/Niro/Dario anchors so the Dario spice -> Kaia field chain reads at floor level.

S5 Current adds broken curb clusters, low fog bands, dead grass clumps, rubble contact scatter, distant ruin silhouette, and a narrow-road walk band. It remains a quiet decline preview and does not include Chapter 2 reveal material.

The Time Window additions are floor and wall adjacency hints only: dust traces, scuff strips, faint inner rim cards, reflection slivers, displaced dust, and era-blend patches. There are no portal rings, thick frames, or physical holes.
